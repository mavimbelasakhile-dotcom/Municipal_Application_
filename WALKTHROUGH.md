# System Walkthrough — City of uMhlathuze Municipal Services Portal

This document is a step-by-step guide through the application, from launch to submitting and reviewing a report. Follow along with the screenshots in the [`screenshots/`](screenshots/) folder.

---

## Step 1 — Launching the Application

When you start the application, the **Main Menu** appears.

**What you see:**
- The red header bar with the municipal call centre and switchboard numbers.
- The **City of uMhlathuze** logo and "Vision Into Action" slogan.
- A live date and time display, and a personalised welcome using your Windows username.
- Three service buttons.

**What you can do:**
- **Report Issues** — active, opens the reporting form.
- **Local Events & Announcements** — disabled (planned for a future release).
- **Service Request Status** — disabled (planned for a future release).
- **View Report History** — opens the list of reports submitted this session.

![Main Menu](screenshots/01-main-menu.png)

> Click **Report Issues** to begin.

---

## Step 2 — The Report Issues Form

The reporting form opens with the same municipal branding for a consistent experience.

**What you see:**
- A progress bar at the top that tracks how complete your report is.
- Input fields on the left: Location, Category, Priority, Description.
- An attachments panel and contact-method panel on the right.

![Report Issues Form](screenshots/02-report-issues-empty.png)

---

## Step 3 — Entering the Location

Start typing an address in the **Location** field. The system suggests matching places in the uMhlathuze area (Richards Bay, Empangeni, eSikhaleni, Nseleni, Ngwelezane, and landmarks).

**How to use it:**
1. Type at least two characters (e.g., "Meer").
2. A suggestion list appears below the field.
3. Click a suggestion, or press the Down arrow and Enter to select.
4. You can also type a custom address if yours is not listed.

![Location Autocomplete](screenshots/03-location-autocomplete.png)

---

## Step 4 — Choosing a Category

Open the **Category** dropdown and select the type of issue you are reporting (for example, Sanitation, Roads & Potholes, Water Supply, Electricity & Power).

![Category Dropdown](screenshots/04-category-dropdown.png)

---

## Step 5 — Setting the Priority

Select a **Priority** level. A colour-coded indicator appears next to the dropdown:

- **Low** — green
- **Medium** — amber
- **High** — orange
- **Critical** — red

![Priority Selector](screenshots/05-priority-selector.png)

---

## Step 6 — Describing the Issue

Type a detailed description in the **Description** box. A live counter shows how many characters you have used out of the 1000 maximum, changing colour as you approach the limit.

![Description](screenshots/06-description.png)

---

## Step 7 — Attaching Supporting Files

Use **+ Attach File(s)** to add photos or documents. Only supported types are allowed: JPG, PNG, BMP, PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT.

**How to use it:**
1. Click **+ Attach File(s)** and choose one or more files.
2. Each attached file shows its name and size in the list.
3. Select a file and click **Remove** to detach it.

![Attachments](screenshots/07-attachments.png)

---

## Step 8 — Choosing How You Want Updates

Pick your **Preferred Contact Method for Updates**:

- **Email** — enter your email address (validated for correct format).
- **Phone Call** — enter your phone number.
- **SMS** — enter your cellphone number.

### Email

![Contact Email](screenshots/08-contact-email.png)

### Phone / SMS

![Contact Phone](screenshots/09-contact-phone.png)

---

## Step 9 — Tracking Your Progress

As you fill in each field, the **progress bar** at the top updates with an encouraging message and changes colour from red to amber to green.

![Progress Bar](screenshots/10-progress-bar.png)

---

## Step 10 — Confirming Submission

When you click **Submit Report**, the system validates your input. If anything is missing or an email/phone is invalid, an error message guides you. Once valid, a **confirmation dialog** summarises your report and asks you to confirm.

![Confirmation Dialog](screenshots/11-confirm-dialog.png)

---

## Step 11 — Submission Success

After confirming, a **success dialog** displays your unique reference number (for example, `UMH-2026-0001`) along with a summary of the report. Save this number to track your issue later.

![Success Dialog](screenshots/12-success-dialog.png)

---

## Step 12 — Reviewing Report History

From the Main Menu, click **View Report History** to see every report submitted this session in a table. Priorities are colour-coded, and double-clicking a row shows the full details.

![Report History](screenshots/13-report-history.png)

---

## Step 13 — Exporting Reports

In the Report History window, click **Export to File** to save all reports to a formatted text file for record-keeping.

![Export](screenshots/14-export.png)

---

## Step 14 — Validation and Feedback

Throughout the process, the application provides clear feedback:

- Required fields must be completed before submission.
- Email addresses are checked for a valid format.
- Phone numbers are checked for a valid length.
- Green indicators appear next to correctly completed fields.

![Validation Error](screenshots/15-validation-error.png)

---

## Summary

The City of uMhlathuze Municipal Services Portal makes it simple for residents to:

1. Report an issue with full detail and supporting files.
2. Choose how they want to be contacted.
3. Receive a unique reference number.
4. Review and export their submitted reports.

All within a consistent, branded, and validated interface.
